            Help and words

            Care queue
            The official list of waiting patients. The top patient is seen first.
            Level 1 to 4
            How urgent a patient is. Level 1 is the most urgent. "Unassessed" means nobody has checked them yet.
            Notes to review
            New or changed notes a nurse still needs to read. Oldest first.
            AI-assisted, unverified
            The AI helper made a suggestion. A person has not checked it yet. It might be wrong.
            Quote
            The exact words from the note. Always compare them with the full note.
            Pin note
            Keeps a note open in a small sticky window while you look at other things.
            Escalate
            Ask the charge nurse for help right away. You never have to wait for the AI.
            Overdue
            A task whose time has passed. It goes to a backup person automatically.
            Charge nurse
            The nurse in charge of the shift. Only they can change a patient's level in this demo.

            Keyboard tips
