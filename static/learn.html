How it works

  1. Pick a scenario and press Start practice. A private practice unit opens with its own patients.
  2. Work the board like a real shift. New notes arrive when you press Next event. Check the AI's evidence against the real note, correct it when it's wrong, check emergency flags and write your reasons, escalate, and set acuity.
  3. Finish to see your score: notes reviewed, AI mistakes caught, emergency calls, reasons, acuity, escalation, and restricted-record access.
  4. Build your own case in My cases: add patients, notes over time, emergencies and the right answers. Practice it yourself, then submit it to your class.

Use only made-up details. The case builder blocks things that look like real names, record numbers, dates of birth, phone numbers or addresses. Your cases are sent to the AI only after an instructor approves them.

Patients

Notes over time

Event 0 = already in the chart at the start. Event 1, 2, 3… arrive each time the learner presses Next event. Words like “demo red flag R” trigger the practice emergency rules; “demo signal A/B” are the terms the AI looks for.
