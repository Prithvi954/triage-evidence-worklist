## Top three, and why

1. **R3: undocumented deterioration.** This is the largest failure technical controls cannot eliminate. A patient who worsens quietly in the waiting room, with no note written, produces no evidence, no inbox item, and no AI signal. The only protection is human: reassessment deadlines that run independently of notes, nurses' direct observation, and patients' or families' ability to raise concerns. **This miss can stay invisible** to the system and to audits based on notes.
2. **R1: accurate quote, wrong interpretation.** This is the failure mode specific to this design. It looks verified, because the quote really is in the note. That is why the full sentence is always shown and corrections are captured.
3. **R2: note text leaking outside the approved path.** In this architecture the most likely privacy failure is not Anthropic itself. It is an adjacent copy: the wrong organization's key, a debug log, an error vendor, or a person pasting a note into chat. These are partly human and only partly testable.
